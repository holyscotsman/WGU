# S5 3 Introduction

## Lesson 3 Introduction

Healthcare is a social institution because it is an organized system of roles, norms, and authority that shapes behavior and influences everyday life. It establishes expectations about illness, treatment, responsibility, and access to care.

In this lesson, you will learn about the sociological perspectives on healthcare as a social institution and how healthcare systems differ around the world.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson.

Identify the main concepts employed in the study of health and medicine.

Describe the three sociological perspectives on health and medicine.

Describe the environmental perspectives on health and medicine.

Identify the differences in healthcare systems around the world.
