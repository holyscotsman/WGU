# Section 2: Summary

## Section 2: Summary

Culture refers to the shared beliefs, values, norms, and practices that shape how individuals understand and participate in society. These components of culture, including symbols, language, values, and norms, provide a framework for behavior and social expectations. Socialization is the process through which individuals learn and internalize culture over time. Through this process, individuals develop a sense of self and learn how to function within social groups and institutions.

## Key Points

Culture and its components: Culture includes shared beliefs, values, norms, symbols, and language that guide behavior and shape social expectations. Processes of culture: Culture is learned and shared through interaction, allowing individuals to participate in society. Socialization and the self: Socialization is the process through which individuals develop identity and learn social roles through interaction with others.
