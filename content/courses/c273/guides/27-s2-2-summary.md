# S2 2 Summary

## Lesson 2: Summary

Take a moment to review what you learned in this lesson.

Culture includes shared components that shape how people think, behave, communicate, and interact in everyday life.

Norms and values are key components of culture that guide behavior and reflect what a society considers important, respectively.

Norms vary in strength and include folkways, mores, and taboos.

People communicate cultural meaning through symbols, language, gestures (nonverbal communication), and material and nonmaterial culture.

Cultural meanings can vary across societies, which can lead to misunderstanding when there is no shared meaning.

Cultural relativism involves understanding beliefs and behaviors within their cultural context, while ethnocentrism involves judging other cultures based on one's own cultural standards.

Culture varies within societies based on factors such as social class, region, and identity, as well as patterns of participation in high culture and popular culture.

Culture changes over time through innovation, diffusion, and globalization, as new ideas and practices are created and spread.
