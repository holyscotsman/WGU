# S3 Lesson 4 Introduction

## Lesson 4 Introduction

When a crime occurs, society must decide how to respond. Sociologists study crime and punishment to understand how laws are enforced, how sanctions are applied, and how these responses affect social groups differently. Crime and punishment are closely connected to social values, power, and inequality, shaping both who is punished and how punishment is experienced. In this lesson, you will examine types of crime, patterns of victimization, and how incarceration is used as a form of punishment in the United States.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson. Identify the different types of crimes. Identify types of punishment and effects of stratification on the criminal justice system.
