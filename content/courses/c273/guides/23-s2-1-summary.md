# S2 1 Summary

## Lesson 1: Summary

Take a moment to think about what you learned in this lesson. Culture is a society's shared beliefs, values, norms, language, symbols, and patterns of behavior. Culture is a process that is created, shared, and changes over time in real-life settings. Culture is shaped within groups, and group membership influences cultural beliefs, values, and behaviors. Biological influences (nature), including reflexes, instincts, and drives, affect human behavior. Sociological influences also shape human behavior. A biological approach, a sociological approach, or both can explain behaviors.
