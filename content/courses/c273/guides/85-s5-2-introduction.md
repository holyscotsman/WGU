# S5 2 Introduction

## Lesson 2 Introduction

Religion and education are powerful social institutions that shape how people understand the world and how societies organize opportunity. Each plays a central role in maintaining stability, transmitting culture, and reproducing or challenging inequality.

In this lesson, you will examine how religion and education function as social institutions, how they shape behavior and social organization, and how sociological perspectives interpret their roles in society. By the end of this lesson, the significance of religion and education in structuring belief systems, opportunity, and social change will be clear.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson:

- Describe the significance of the institutions of religion.
- Describe the significance of the institutions of education.
