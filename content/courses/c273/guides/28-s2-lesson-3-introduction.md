# S2 Lesson 3 Introduction

## Lesson 3 Introduction

From the moment people are born, they begin learning how to function as members of society. In sociology, this process is known as socialization, and it plays a central role in the development of the self.

In this lesson, you will explore how socialization occurs through interactions with family, peers, institutions, and media, including social media, and how it continues across the lifespan. You will also examine how socialization plays a central role in the development of the self as it shapes identity, including gender roles, gender identity, and sexual behaviors.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson:

Identify the agents of socialization.

Identify the life course process.

Explain the social construction of gender.

Identify different sexual identities and how social context shapes human sexual behaviors.

## Key

socialization: learning the norms, values, beliefs, and behaviors of your society self: a sense of who you are that is formed and shaped by interactions with others and society
