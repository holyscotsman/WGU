# S4 1 Introduction

## Lesson 1 Introduction

When you look at the world around you, it is easy to notice differences in how people live. Some neighborhoods have well-funded schools and new businesses, while others struggle with limited resources and fewer opportunities. There are disparities in living conditions across countries with respect to income, infrastructure, and global power. Why do these differences exist?

In this lesson, you will examine how societies organize people into different layers based on access to resources and opportunities. You will explore how inequality operates within the United States and around the world and consider different explanations for why these patterns continue over time.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson:

- Identify systems of social stratification in different societies.
- Identify types, causes, and consequences of poverty and social inequality.
- Identify stratification and inequality between nations and global systems.
