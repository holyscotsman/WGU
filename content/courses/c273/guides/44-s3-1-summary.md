# S3 1 Summary

## Lesson 1: Summary

Take a moment to think about what you learned in this lesson. Groups play an important role in shaping behavior and social organization in society. Sociologists define groups based on regular interaction and shared identity, which distinguishes them from nongroups. Primary and secondary groups influence individuals and society in different ways based on closeness and purpose. In-groups and out-groups shape identity, belonging, and how people relate to others. Reference groups influence attitudes, goals, and behavior by providing standards for comparison. Groups influence behavior by establishing norms and encouraging people to follow them through social pressure and authority. Statuses organize individuals within society, including the multiple statuses people hold and those that most strongly define identity. Roles guide behavior by setting expectations tied to positions individuals occupy in groups.
