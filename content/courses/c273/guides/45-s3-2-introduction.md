# S3 2 Introduction

## Lesson 2 Introduction

People spend much of their lives in groups, from families and work teams to schools and organizations. These groups vary in size, structure, and formality, and those differences shape how people interact, make decisions, and behave.

In this lesson, you will examine how group size, leadership, and group behavior influence how people interact and make decisions. You will also explore formal organizations and rule-based systems to understand how large, structured groups operate in modern society.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through this lesson:

- Identify types of groups, types of leadership, and group phenomena.
- Identify types of organizations and their growth in modern society.
