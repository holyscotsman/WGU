# Section 4: Summary

## Section 4 Summary

Social inequality is shaped by systems that organize individuals into hierarchies based on access to resources, power, and status. Stratification systems vary across societies, but all influence life chances and social mobility. Race, ethnicity, sex, gender, and sexuality each play a role in shaping social experiences, often creating patterns of advantage and disadvantage. Understanding these structures helps explain how inequality is maintained and experienced in everyday life.

## Key Points

Stratification systems and inequality: Societies organize people into hierarchical systems that affect access to resources, opportunities, and social mobility. Race and ethnicity in society: Race and ethnicity shape social experiences by influencing identity, group membership, and patterns of inequality. Sex, gender, and sexuality in society: Social expectations related to sex, gender, and sexuality influence roles, access to opportunities, and experiences of inequality.
