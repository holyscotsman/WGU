# S4 2 Introduction

## Lesson 2 Introduction

When you fill out a form, you may be asked to identify your race or ethnicity. These categories often seem straightforward, but have you ever wondered who decided what they mean or why they matter? Throughout history, societies have grouped people in different ways, and those groupings have shaped how people are treated.

In this lesson, you will examine how societies define and use racial and ethnic categories and how those definitions influence access to power and opportunity. You will explore how unequal treatment develops and how it can operate in everyday interactions, institutions, and larger social systems.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson.

Distinguish the social construction of race, ethnicity, and modern racial categories.

Identify the sociological explanations of prejudice and discrimination.

Identify the sociological explanations of racism.
