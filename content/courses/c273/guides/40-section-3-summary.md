# Section 3: Summary

## Section 3 Summary

Conformity and deviance describe how individuals respond to social norms within a society. Social groups and organizations play a key role in establishing, reinforcing, and regulating these norms through shared expectations and structures. The dynamics within groups influence how individuals behave and whether they conform or deviate from accepted standards. Sociologists use different explanations and examine patterns of crime and deviance across social groups to understand how and why deviance occurs.

## Key Points

Groups and social norms: Social groups and organizations establish and enforce norms that guide individual behavior. Group dynamics: Interactions within groups influence whether individuals conform to or deviate from social expectations. Deviance and social patterns: Sociologists study explanations of deviance and analyze patterns of crime across social groups to understand differences in behavior.
