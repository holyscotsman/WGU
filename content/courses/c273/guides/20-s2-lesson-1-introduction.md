# S2 Lesson 1 Introduction

## Lesson 1 Introduction

Every day, you follow rules and expectations, often without even thinking about them. For example, when someone asks, "How are you?" you probably respond with a short, polite answer rather than sharing every detail of your day. These shared beliefs and values are part of the concept called culture. In this lesson, you will learn what culture is and how it shapes the way people think, communicate, and behave. You will explore how culture is created and shared within groups and how it can change over time. You will also examine how both biology and the social environment influence behavior.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson. Identify the cultural outcomes of processes and groups. Explain biological and sociological approaches to behavior.
