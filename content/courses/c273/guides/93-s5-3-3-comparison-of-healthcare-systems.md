## U.S. Healthcare System

The United States has a mixed public-private healthcare system. Coverage is provided through a combination of government programs and private insurance, which makes the system complex and often debated.

## Public Insurance Programs

Medicare provides coverage for people age 65 and older and some individuals with disabilities. Medicaid serves low-income individuals who meet eligibility requirements. Other public programs include the Veterans Health Administration, the Indian Health Service, and the Children's Health Insurance Program.

## Private Insurance

Most private coverage is provided through employers. Some individuals purchase insurance directly from private companies. Despite these options, millions of Americans remain uninsured or underinsured, meaning they lack adequate financial protection for healthcare costs.

Affordable Care Act (ACA) Passed in 2010 and fully implemented in 2014, the ACA expanded Medicaid eligibility and prohibited the denial of coverage for pre-existing conditions. The law increased insurance coverage for millions of Americans, but debates about cost, access, and government involvement continue. Key takeaway: The U.S. has a mixed public (Medicare, Medicaid) and private (employer health insurance) healthcare system.

## Systems

What distinguishes the Beveridge model from the Bismarck model of healthcare? The Beveridge model limits access to those who can afford private insurance, while the Bismarck model guarantees universal access through government funding. The Beveridge model provides universal access funded through taxes, while the Bismarck model provides access primarily through employmentbased insurance contributions.

The Beveridge model provides access only to older adults, while the Bismarck model provides access only to unemployed individuals. The Beveridge model requires direct out-of-pocket payment for most services, while the Bismarck model eliminates patient costs entirely.

## Answer

Correct: The Beveridge model offers universal access financed through taxation, whereas the Bismarck model ties access to insurance funded by employer and employee contributions.

## Key Terms

Beveridge model: healthcare system funded through taxes in which the government provides healthcare services Bismarck model: healthcare system funded through employer and employee insurance contributions national health insurance model: healthcare system in which the government operates a national insurance program that pays private providers for healthcare services out-of-pocket: direct payment by an individual for healthcare services without insurance coverage or government assistance
