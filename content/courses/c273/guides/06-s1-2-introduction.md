# S1 2 Introduction

## Lesson 2 Introduction

Why do some people see society as mostly stable and working as it should, while others see it as unfair and shaped by power struggles? The answer often depends on the perspective someone uses to interpret what is happening. In sociology, perspectives act like lenses that help explain patterns in social life, from how schools function, to why inequality exists, to how everyday interactions create shared meaning.

In this lesson, you will explore three major sociological perspectives and their associated theories: functional perspective, conflict perspective, symbolic interactionist perspective, and feminist theory. You will also examine how sociology can be used in practical ways through applied and clinical sociology to better understand and improve real-world social conditions.

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson:

- Identify the functionalist, conflict, and symbolic interactionist perspectives.
- Define applied sociology and real-life applications of sociology.
