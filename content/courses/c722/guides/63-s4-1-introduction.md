# S4 1 Introduction

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson:

Contrast common project management methods.

Identify the five processes for managing a project.

Describe traditional project management methods.

Describe agile project management methods.

Describe extreme project management methods.

## Overview

This lesson will introduce the PMBOK 7 Development Approach and Life Cycle performance domain. This domain is a method used to create and evolve the product during its project life cycle, such as predictive, iterative, or adaptive.

This lesson will build on the knowledge of how the project life cycle describes the doing of project work to the managing of the project itself. There are many processes required to manage a project, and they are organized into five process groups. The groups will be defined, and examples of activities included in each group will be provided.

This lesson will also provide a comparison of traditional, agile, and extreme project management approaches. While this course focuses primarily on the predictive traditional approaches—which follow the chronological life cycle stages: define, plan, execute, close—there are other approaches you can use when the project outcomes or environment are less certain.
