# S2 2 Summary

## Summary

In this lesson, you examined the triple constraints of a project—scope, cost, and time—and how they impact the success or failure of a project. Using various scenarios, the lesson illustrated how changing one side of the triple constraint triangle impacts the other sides. Key to any conversation about the triple constraint is how quality impacts the project. Quality requirements impact cost and often time. The lesson discussed scope creep and the importance of minimizing it so that a project does not get off track. In addition, the lesson discussed expenses that should be directly charged to the project.

The old saying goes, “You can have it fast, cheap, or good, but you cannot have all three.” A project manager’s role is to find a way to have it all—a quality product or service that meets all requirements (scope), is delivered in a specified amount of time (schedule), and produced at or under budget (cost). It is important to understand how the triple constraints act and react to one another and reflect the possible success of meeting the customer’s expectations.
