# S7 1 Summary

## Summary

In this lesson, you looked at project teams. You saw that teams can accomplish more than any individual or more than a group of individuals working on their own. Teams, you learned, happen through nature and give an example of what can be accomplished working in a coordinated fashion.

You learned about two common types of teams: colocated and virtual. Colocated teams are the more traditional team format. This is where all team members are located in the same location. Virtual teams have members spread over a vast geographic area, possibly globally. Each type of team has advantages and disadvantages.

All teams go through growing pains. This is a series of five stages that all teams progress through—forming, storming, norming, performing, adjourning—from the initial formation through learning to work together and finally to the completion of the project and adjournment. Teams can regress to prior stages anytime the interpersonal dynamics in the team changes, for any reason. The team leader may be able to help accelerate the team formation and mitigate some regression, but all teams go through this process. Virtual teams have the additional challenge of not having "face-to-face" communication. This may exacerbate some of the team development and lengthen some of the stages of team formation.
