# S5 1 Introduction

Learning objectives:

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson:

Describe the purpose of project planning.

Describe how the scope of a project is developed.

Describe common tools used to assign project team roles.

"Great things do not simply happen. They must be brought to pass through effort. They must also achieve their initial existence in the minds of visionaries before they achieve physical reality.

## Projects do not often succeed without careful planning." 1

"Planning is essential to controlling a project. Planning establishes a baseline with which to gauge progress. If the project begins to deviate from the plan, action can be taken to put the project back on track. Without planning, there is no control." 2

In this lesson, you will see the processes, tools, and techniques required to plan a project put into action. The purpose of planning is described. Finalizing the project charter will aid in understanding the transition from the defining phase to the planning phase. You will recognize the integration of various performance domains such as Delivery, Team, Development Approach, Planning, and Uncertainty as the project scope and scope statement are developed. Finally, the tools used to assign project team roles will be addressed.
