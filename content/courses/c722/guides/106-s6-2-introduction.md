# S6 2 Introduction

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson: Describe the components of an activity network including their sequencing. Construct a project network diagram. Describe task relationships and dependencies in network diagrams. Describe how a Gantt chart is used in project scheduling.

Once you move through your requirement planning and gain a clear understanding of deliverables, create a work breakdown structure (WBS). Take the activities and tasks identified during the WBS process and expand them as your knowledge increases through your planning process. Details that can be added include the following:

- further definitions of tasks through requirement clarification
- budgeting for tasks in time and money

resource recommendations of the type of resource, including human resources and materials relationships between each task and associated activity, including sequence or precedence This lesson will introduce the process used by project managers to organize this information into a graphic representation or a network diagram that visually shows the team and stakeholders how the schedule will unfold. The goal is to develop a clear understanding of what will be done, when it will be done, in what order it will be done, and how these activities or tasks interrelate with each other.
