# S6 2 Summary

## Summary

In this lesson, you learned to take the information from the work breakdown structure to organize your activities into a schedule. Key to this is taking the activities and defining the relationships across them. You begin to understand the dependencies and how they impact your ability to get everything done on time. Once you understand the relationships, you can determine which order you will do the activities and tasks. In this drawing, you begin to get enough information to create a network diagram, a critical path, and a Gantt chart for the schedule. You also will do a check once more on how complete the activity and task list are and how well you will meet the project requirements. You follow your plan and incorporate the requirements into your detail charts. If changes or new knowledge offers the need for iterative planning, you need to go back and review the planning as described in other changes. Rolling wave or iterative planning may occur, and then these charts may require changes.
