# S3 1 Summary

## Summary

In this lesson, you looked at the importance of selecting the best projects for companies in order to make the most out of limited resources. Project selection is often carried out by senior management, in coordination with representatives from the project management office. The PMBOK 7 guide provided a set of tools to assist in the project selection process using models, methods, and artifacts. Project selection decisions may be made using either numeric or non-numeric models, which allows you to compare competing project proposals to each other and create a portfolio that best addresses the company's strategic needs. Consider projects you have been involved with at work or through other organizations you are familiar with and what selection methods were likely used for those projects. Have you ever seen a necessary project not be approved or an unimportant project given priority? Why do you think that happened? What could have been done to avoid the situation?
