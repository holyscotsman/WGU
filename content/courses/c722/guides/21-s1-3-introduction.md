# S1 3 Introduction

## Learning Objectives

Describe the advantages and disadvantages of common types of organizational structures. Explain the advantages of a project management office (PMO).

## Lesson 3: Types of Projects

You will explore the advantages and disadvantages of organizational structures as well as the role, responsibility, and authority of the project manager in each type of project:

- functional
- matrix
- projectized
- You will learn about the project management office by
- learning the advantages,
- determining an organization's project management maturity level, and

seeing the benefits of PMO. Good luck in your project management endeavors.
