# S2 1.5 The Project Life Cycle Activity- Project Defining Phase Activities

## Learning Objectives

Describe the activities of each phase of a project.

## Define your target. Shutterstock

Remember that the project defining phase is when an idea is developed into a project proposal. To bring clarity to the idea, the project organization will need to work closely with the customer to develop a shared understanding of the intended project outcomes. The process of gathering information from the customer and stakeholders to define the high-level project outcomes is called collecting customer requirements. Understanding the customer requirements will help the project team determine which processes and activities need to occur to complete the project. For example, as you create your restaurant, you might have a great idea for the menu and you have some lively conversations with your chef. But when you start to collect the ideas, it becomes clear that the menu needs to be well defined and the market you will serve needs to be clear. A requirement of the project becomes an activity to finalize the menu and define the market to be served.

Customer requirements are then refined into measurable goals and specifications. Measurable goals may include the following:

- the date that a product needs to be ready for release to the market
- the price point that will be achieved
- the maximum production time required for assembly
- the total cost of the project
- the features and functionality
- similar quantifiable results

The measures chosen can vary depending on the type of project, but the use of measurable goals serves to keep the project team focused on outcomes that meet or exceed the customer’s expectations. In the case of your restaurant, an example of the goal could be the following: Create a menu that will be well received by the customer and yield revenue averaging $30 per customer with a 50% margin and that uses locally grown produce.

The term specifications are often used to describe customer requirements that identify precise and measurable characteristics of the project outcome. Specifications are often thought of in terms of design and engineering requirements such as the finished weight of a product, physical dimensions, processing speed, response time for software and processes, or similar measurable characteristics of the project outcome.

## Preliminary Project Scope

Triple constraints triangle with Cost on the bottom left, Time on the bottom right, Scope at the top, and Quality in the center of the triangle.

Goal and specification information is used to write the preliminary scope of the project. At this point, the preliminary project scope is a statement written at a high level, summarizing the work that will be completed to provide a project outcome that will meet the customer or sponsor’s requirements. The preliminary scope will be further refined during the detailed planning phase, but a preliminary project scope statement is developed as an outcome of the defining phase, which will serve as a guide to the project team as they plan the project activities. The customer or sponsor and the organization defining the project must agree on the project preliminary scope statement within the project charter and stated deliverables before the project planning phase can begin. Once the initial project scope is agreed upon, the project team will use it to focus their planning efforts, so it needs to be comprehensive enough that the team understands the requirements and how they can be achieved. Only then can they do work breakdown and divide up the work and create a realistic budget and schedule.

Who and Where Is the Customer? The role of the customer is central to project management. The customer is the person or organization providing the requirements that define the project outcomes receiving the benefits of the project. The customer may be part of the same company providing the project team (internal customer), or the project team and customer may be in two separate companies (external customer). If the Cobalt Blue Manufacturing Company needs a new piece of equipment installed, Cobalt Blue would be identified as the customer of the equipment installation project. Cobalt Blue may identify a specific individual in their company who will represent their interests and be the primary contact for the project team—possibly the head of manufacturing. If the project manager and her team come from an external engineering company, Cobalt Blue would be considered an external customer since the project team and the customer are from two different companies. When the project team and the customer are part of the same company, then the project has an internal customer. It is not unusual for a project to be run in-house. In either case, the project team needs to understand the customer’s requirements and will work to deliver the project outcomes and deliverables to meet the customer’s expectations.

## Project Justification

The project justification is a statement explaining the business need for the project. This is sometimes referred to as the business case. The business case helps to justify the project by tying the project back to the strategic and operational goals of the company and its mission. If a company wants to increase its revenue by 15% next year, a project proposal may be justified by its potential contribution to that revenue goal.

## Project Selection Process

During the defining stage, the customer and project organization must determine whether the project is worthy of moving forward. At this point, enough information has been gathered to generate a project proposal outlining the costs/budget, benefits, method, scope, and justification that the project's viability can be determined. The project selection process presents methods of evaluating project proposals on which proposed project or projects will yield the highest benefit or profitability. If it is determined that the project benefits are worth the costs, the project will move forward. Once the business case and project charter are developed and approved, the project will move on to the planning phase where a detailed project plan will be developed.

The Project Sponsor The person in the project organization who has authority to expend resources for projects will likely be the project sponsor. The project sponsor will sign the project charter, which summarizes all the key information about the project and authorizes the project manager to assemble the team and begin detailed planning. The issuance of the project charter officially ends the defining phase of the project life cycle.
