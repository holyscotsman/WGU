# S7 4 Introduction

## Learning Objectives

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson: Describe how to close a project. Describe the steps in performing a lessons learned review, as part of project closeout.

## Overview

Eventually, every project comes to an end. The deliverables are completed and ready to be transferred to the customer. Carefully managing the closing phase is important as any other phase in the project life cycle. Closing out a project is a formal process, and there are several tasks. The major task is to ensure the project is approved and accepted by the customer. Project closure will focus on the element of the PMBOK 7 performance domains of Stakeholder, Team, Development Approach and Life Cycle, and Delivery.

## Stakeholder

The goal of project management is to deliver value to the stakeholders. While deliverables are a means to this end, the true measure of success lies in the value and benefits these deliverables provide to stakeholders. Therefore, the ultimate indicator of a project’s success is stakeholder satisfaction.

## Team

A few key actions toward the end of an iteration or project are recognizing team members’ contributions and capturing retrospectives or lessons learned. Team members should be recognized for their work and any professional growth they have experienced while on the project. Lessons learned can refer to both tacit and explicit knowledge and should be captured so that future teams can refer to them for guidance. When done well, the organization gains knowledge through the experience of performing projects.

## Development Approach and Life Cycle/Delivery

If you use the predictive or an adaptive life cycle, project success will be based on whether you meet the stakeholders' needs. A successful project may benefit the organization well beyond the project’s close. In the next two lessons, you will discover the artifacts, tools, and techniques required to formally close a project. You will also examine the steps to perform a lesson learned review as part of the project closeout.
