## Choosing Which Model(s) to Use

The PMBOK 7 guide provides a set of tools to assist in management of projects with models, methods, and artifacts. Model: A model is a thinking strategy to explain a process, framework, or phenomenon. Method: A method is the means for achieving an outcome, output, result, or project. Artifact: An artifact can be a template, document, output, or project deliverable.

Models can move the focus of project evaluation and prioritization, depending on the model's purpose. As mentioned earlier, models may focus on profit, return on investment, time required to return the initial investment, market share, or a host of other non-numeric outcomes. Which model(s) to use depends on the goals of the company. However, there is a framework to ensure that the models you consider using follow best practices.
