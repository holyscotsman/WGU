# S7 1 Introduction

## Learning Objective

Consider the following learning objectives as you move through the lesson:

Describe the characteristics of an effective project team.

## Overview

PMBOK 7 considers the Team performance domain a critical one, as it interacts with all other performance domains. The project manager’s leadership focuses on motivating, listening, enabling, and influencing; everyone in the project team should practice all these functions.

However, management activities can be either centralized or distributed (in self-managed teams). The tailoring leadership style would depend on the maturity of the project team members, organization structure, distributed teams (virtual or geographically disbursed), and experience with the type of project at hand.

In this lesson, you will understand the characteristics of an effective and high-performing team, the stages of team development, and developing teams in colocated and virtual teams.
