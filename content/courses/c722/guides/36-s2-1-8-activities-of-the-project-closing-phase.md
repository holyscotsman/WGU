# S2 1.8 Activities of the Project Closing Phase

## Learning Objectives

Describe the activities of each phase of a project.

## Activities of the Project Closing Phase

This phase finalizes all activities of the project. The activities of project closure can be grouped into three areas. The first step is to turn the project's final deliverables over to the customer or sponsor. The second step is to close out all the procurements. In the third step, you complete the administrative work to close out the project.

Deliver Project Outcomes and Deliverables to the Customer or Sponsor After internally verifying that the project's final deliverables meet the quality standards required, it is turned over to the customer or sponsor for them to evaluate and officially accept. Confirm project final deliverables match customer or sponsor requirements. Turn over finished project deliverables to the customer or sponsor. Provide any documentation to the customer or sponsor. Obtain customer or sponsor acceptance and sign-off on the project outcome. Get feedback from the customer or sponsor.

## Close All Procurement Activity

Any products or services obtained from outside of the project team must be paid for or returned for credit. Since the project accounts and the project team will no longer be available at closure, all invoices must be paid and credits received. Pay all open invoices.

Return excess materials. Manage disposition of leased equipment. Process all credits for returns and adjustments. Reconcile all accounts payable. Complete final financial reports required.

## Complete the Administrative Closeout

Since the project work is complete, all documentation must be updated, and any remaining assets must be transferred back to the sponsoring organization. Complete team member performance reports. Archive all records. Gather and document lessons learned. Return all assets to the sponsoring organization. Release team members to new assignments. When all of these steps are completed, the project is officially over. It is time to move on to defining, planning, and executing the next project.
