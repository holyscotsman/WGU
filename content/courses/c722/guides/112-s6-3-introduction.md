# S6 3 Introduction

## Learning Objectives

Determine the critical path. Describe schedule compression techniques.

## Overview

In this lesson, you will discover how the critical path is constructed and understand the schedule compression. You will understand how the critical path can answer very important questions such as "How long will the project take to complete?" You will also recognize float or slack times in a project, as well as calculate start and finish times using the forward and backward pass. You will also understand how techniques such as crashing and fast tracking are used to compress the schedule if there is a change in the project schedule due to several reasons—one being delays in project tasks.
