# S4 1.5 The Five Processes for Managing a Project- Closing Process

## Learning Objectives

Identify the five processes for managing a project.

## Early Project Termination

Part of the monitoring process is to ensure that the project outcomes are still needed by the customer relative to the time and cost required. It is possible that during the monitoring process, it is determined that the project outcomes are no longer needed or resources are not available. In that case, the project work would stop immediately, and the team would move to the project closing processes. Imagine that you are the project manager for a team that is developing a new retail product. The product concept is a lightweight headphone set that plays both broadcast radio and internet music services. The team is doing great, and you are 80% finished with developing the working prototype. Both the schedule and the budget are being met as planned. However, your top competitor just released that exact product at a lower cost than your project had targeted. It would not be a good use of company resources to continue development of a similar product at a higher cost. After consultation with the project sponsor and the customer, the decision is made to cancel the project. The team should immediately stop executing the project plan and move to the activities associated with closing the project.

## The Processes of Closing

The processes related to closing the project involve all of the specific actions that need to occur to ensure that all project activities are completed. These activities wrap up all the project work so that the team can move on to other projects. Processes occurring during project closure include the following:

- paying all outstanding vendor invoices
- reconciling all vendor accounts

returning excess materials and supplies returning all project assets to the sponsoring organization writing the lessons learned completing all project documentation delivering the completed project to the customer receiving sign-off from the customer archiving all project documents
