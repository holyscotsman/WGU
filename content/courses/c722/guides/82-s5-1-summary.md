# S5 1 Summary

## Summary

This lesson discussed the need for planning, its purpose, the project charter, and other planning documents. You saw that without a plan, you do not know how you are going to achieve the project objectives on time and on budget with the required quality. A plan can help you identify issues, such as being over budget and behind schedule, and can help determine resolution. Next, you looked at the project scope. You saw the purpose of the scope and how the integration of the various performance domains as suggested by PMBOK 7: Delivery, Team, Development Approach and Life Cycle, Planning, Measurement, and Uncertainty are integrated in developing the project scope and project scope statement. You saw who is involved in developing the scope and how it helps determine detailed requirements and how the requirements are developed. You should have gained an understanding of why requirements are developed. Then you looked at defining team member roles and responsibilities (R&R). The need for welldefined R&R was explained, as well as the need for a skills matrix, where the skills required for each task are specified. Knowledge, skills, and abilities (KSAs) were defined, and an understanding of why knowing these are important was provided. The use of a resource histogram was explained, and the value of the histogram in assigning resources became clear. You also saw how a histogram may assist in moving resources from less critical areas to more critical areas to keep projects on schedule.
