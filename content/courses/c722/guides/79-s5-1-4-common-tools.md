# S5 1.4 Common Tools

## Learning Objectives

Describe common tools used to assign project team roles.

## Resource Responsibility Matrix

The required items of education, experience, skills, and abilities are dependent on the specific roles and responsibilities of each project team member. For these roles and responsibilities to be determined, first the project scope and project requirements must be defined. From this, the resources needed are identified and the roles and responsibilities are detailed in a resource responsibility matrix. A sample responsibility matrix is shown below.

## A

As you can see, the left-hand column will list all of the activities required to complete the project. This list of activities was developed in the work breakdown structure (WBS). At this point, you will identify who will be working on each of the activities and their role and level of responsibility in each activity. At a minimum, two levels of responsibility are identified. These are often identified as either a responsible or a supportive role. The responsible role coordinates activities of all members involved in carrying out the activity and ensures that all resources are available. Identifying responsibility is very important because this is the person who has the task of ensuring, among other items, all resources are available in the proper quantity and at the proper time. This includes people as well as equipment and facilities. This is the person tasked with coordinating all resources and communications between and among all relevant project personnel. The supportive role will generally carry out the activities or provide administrative support. This role is often further clarified as to the type of support. This may be administrative, laboratory, engineering, and so on. In addition to identifying responsibilities, the roles and responsibility matrix is an aid in identifying activities that may not have adequate personnel assigned. The roles and responsibilities matrix will often name the specific individuals who will fill each role. This will assist later in ensuring that the required individual and expertise are available as needed. The resource histogram is a valuable tool for this. In addition to the roles and responsibilities matrix, it is often helpful to develop a matrix showing the required skills for each role and the available skills for each person available for the project team. This aids in ensuring that the personnel assigned to each role have the necessary skills and experience required. It can also indicate skills that may be lacking and which personnel may need additional training. A sample skill matrix follows.

## Skill Matrix

A skill matrix is a matrix showing the required skills for each role and the available skills for each person available for the project team. A sample skill matrix is given in which there are five main columns and six rows.
