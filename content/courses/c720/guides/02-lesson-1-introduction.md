# Lesson 1 Introduction

The foundational concepts in operations are defined as the processes through which people, capital, and material are combined to produce the services and goods consumed by the public. Service operations and manufacturing operations have many similarities, but one important difference: goods are tangible, and services are intangible. Successful operations management requires teamwork among operations and other functional areas within an organization. Organizations must compete within the constraints presented by their external environment, which include competitors, economic conditions, government regulations, ethics, and sustainability.

## Learning Objective

Consider the following learning objective as you move through the lesson:

Describe the challenges of ethical and social responsibilities for sustainability related to operations and the environment.
