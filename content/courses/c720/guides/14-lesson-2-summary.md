# Lesson 2 Summary

Take a moment to reflect on what you have learned in this lesson: Satisfying customer requirements means building competitive capabilities such as flexibility, productivity, quality, and time. The four key processes within organizations that are substantially impacted by operations are strategy development, product development, development of systems to produce services and goods, and order fulfillment. A SWOT analysis is a high-level method of evaluation for improving profits, increasing market share, and developing new markets. Operations can be designed and used to gain competitive advantage and increase productivity. Productivity is the evaluation of outputs, like profit, divided by the inputs, like labor and materials.
