# Lesson 3 Introduction

Proper inventory management can help businesses meet demand and work more efficiently. For many items, it does not make sense to produce that item only when there is demand. Managing inventory properly involves analyzing demand patterns. Demand patterns can help manage risk, predict future demand, and identify opportunities. There are many types of inventories that will help a manager ensure that they have knowledge of what items are on hand. As a part of good inventory management, inventory counted at appropriately established intervals will be most beneficial for any organization.

The established inventory count intervals depend on the sensitivity of the items, the value of the items, and whether there is a constant need to know the status of the items. Perpetual inventory is for the most important and critical inventory items when there is a need to know the items' status at all times. Periodic inventory is for items where the manager does not constantly need to know the items' status. The categorization of the value and sensitivity of items is conducted by an ABC analysis, which will determine the level of control and the inventory count interval needed for each item.

## Learning Objective

Consider the following learning objective as you move through the lesson:

Identify the advantages and disadvantages of perpetual or periodic inventory systems.
