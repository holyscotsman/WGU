## Section 2: Introduction

Quality management helps an organization achieve greater consistency of tasks and activities involved in producing goods and services. Quality management increases the efficiency of processes and improves customer satisfaction. High-quality products and services are often qualifiers and award-winners, whereas poor-quality products and services can adversely impact a customer's

experience, a business's reputation, or one's own career. Customers' perception of quality is paramount to an organization's success, and the importance of managing quality cannot be overstated. Operations management is the transformation of customer expectations into products or services. Quality is considered from the perspective of an organization's internal processes and external customers. Two major strategic quality management methodologies are Total Quality Management (TQM) and Six Sigma. Within these two primary methodologies, there are many systems that can assist in the discovery of quality defects or errors. A few of these tools include the PDCA cycle, benchmarking, and process control charts and graphs. Simply put, quality management is a key to customer satisfaction and a key to good operations.

## Build Employability Skills and Competence

Quality management is an integral part of any organization achieving its goals relating to profitability and sustainability. It requires cooperation and collaboration across different functions of the business, along with an understanding of the basic concepts, methods, and tools used to create a culture of quality within an organization. In this course, you can demonstrate these skills by passing a final assessment that measures whether you are proficient in the following competency: The learner explains appropriate quality management strategies for continuous improvement in an organization.
