Within operational strategies, operations play a critical role because they provide the means through which organizations provide the services andStatistics goods consumed by the public. Organizations must

determine the strategies necessary to support the efficient management of the business processes

involved in providing these services and goods. The following are the key points to remember from this section: Operations is the processes through which people, capital, and material are combined to produce the services and goods consumed by the public. Organizations must compete within the constraints presented by their external environment. Organizations have ethical and social responsibilities to their employees and society to provide goods and services that support the "triple bottom line" of economic profitability, social responsibility, and environmental viability. Satisfying customer requirements means building competitive capabilities, such as flexibility and productivity. The four key processes within organizations are strategy development, product development, development of systems to produce services and goods, and order fulfillment. A strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats (SWOT) analysis is a high-level method of evaluation. Operations can be designed and used in a manner to increase productivity, which is the evaluation of outputs like profit divided by inputs like labor and materials. The purpose of inventory within an organization is to address different demand pattern requirements relating to each item or group of items. There are several common types of inventories recognized within organizations for inventory management. The advantages and disadvantages relating to perpetual and periodic inventory systems must be considered when deciding the appropriate method for managing an inventory item. ABC analysis is used to determine the level of inventory control for items based on the value and importance of each item. There are four inventory costs. The primary inventory costs that an organization focuses on are ordering costs, carrying costs, and stockout costs, while the quantity discount model (QDM) considers purchasing costs. The economic order quantity (EOQ) model determines when ordering costs and carrying costs are equal to minimize total costs, while economic production quantity (EPQ) considers production setup costs when determining the minimum total costs. The use of safety stock is a key method in preventing stockouts.
