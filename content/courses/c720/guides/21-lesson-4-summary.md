# Lesson 4 Summary

Take a moment to reflect on what you have learned in this lesson: The primary inventory costs that an organization focuses on are ordering costs, carrying costs, and stockout costs. The economic order quantity (EOQ) model determines when ordering costs and carrying costs are equal to minimize total costs. The quantity discount model (QDM) considers purchasing costs when determining the minimum total costs. The economic production quantity (EPQ) considers production setup costs when determining the minimum total costs. The use of safety stock is a key method in preventing stockouts.
